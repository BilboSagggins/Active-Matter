                      Tips & Tricks

                      Community-tested habits for getting loot home. A raid only pays if you reach the Shelter, so most of these are about knowing when to stop.

                      Before you drop

                      • Pick one goal per raid: weapons, crafting parts, Active Matter, valuables, or an investigation item.
                      • Read the objective panel and map right after spawning. Check whether the loot spot is on your route to extraction.
                      • Learn new maps in Isolated Raids (no enemy squads), then take that route into Open Raids.
                      • Carry a shotgun as your second gun for barges and tight interiors. Devourers eat automatic ammo.
                      • Bring fire to Park. It works well on Flowermen and Dendroids, and Molotovs now ignite instantly.

                      While looting

                      • Always grab Active Matter. It has its own capacity and doesn't take backpack space.
                      • Don't pick Active Matter off lure flowers until you're ready to fight; it wakes the nest.
                      • Search backpacks lying around (Downtown trains especially). They can hold keys and valuables.
                      • Check vehicle trunks: Ural trucks, the Headquarters pickup, Downtown cars and Dalniy Island cars all roll loot.
                      • Rare-loot zones show as color-coded resource icons on the raid map. Monster clusters there mean better loot.
                      • Small monster habitats no longer respawn on their own; a rift next to each one summons them. Clear and move.

                      Getting out

                      • Found something hard to replace? Treat the rest of the raid as an extraction run.
                      • Low on meds, ammo or armor? Skip side buildings and take the quieter exit.
                      • After a loud fight in an Open Raid, avoid the obvious extraction approach.
                      • Dogorsk uses dynamic extraction points. Don't follow an old route from memory.

                      Inventory & space while looting

                      • Empty backpacks fold. An empty backpack takes much less volume, so loot spare packs empty and fill them only when you swap.
                      • Safe container overflow. One item bigger than the container's capacity still fits if it's the only thing inside. Use it for a bulky gun or valuable that wouldn't otherwise fit.
                      • Safe container = what hurts to lose. Keys, investigation items and top valuables go there; its contents teleport to your Shelter if you die. In a squad, wait to evacuate it if a teammate can still revive you.
                      • Active Matter is free space. It has its own capacity, so always pick it up.
                      • Rigs and sleeves shrink consumables. Magazine rigs cut ammo and grenade volume (Large Magazine Rig −30%), and many body sleeves cut ammo, grenade or medicine volume by 10–20%.
                      • Medkits → Medications. Break a medkit down into a Medications pile: one slot of volume restores 100 medkit charges.
                      • Carry ammo in mags. Loose ammo boxes are heavy and bulky; load what you need before the raid.
                      • Small, high-value first. Notes and documents weigh almost nothing, cassette recorders and books sell for about 1,000 credits each, and weapon recipe components take less space than the gun.
                      • Heavy loot near the exit only. Gas cans are worth about 1,000 credits but are very heavy; grab them on the way out.
                      • Swap, don't hoard. Drop low-value items when something better shows up, and do your reshuffling behind cover, not next to the container.
                      • Meds in quick-access pockets. Pulling them from the backpack adds a delay mid-fight.
                      • Backpacks crafted in a raid go straight into an empty slot or container, and only drop to the ground if you're out of room.
                      • Swapping rigs: your items stay in the old rig, and the new one keeps whatever its owner carried, so check it before dropping yours.
                      • Offload mid-raid. Quad bike trunks send their contents to the Shelter when the bike reaches an exit, and a Robot Dog can extract items on its own.
                      • Empty the temporary stash after a raid. Items evacuated by the safe container or Robot Dog go there, and it clears when your next raid starts.

                      Back at the Shelter

                      • Check whether an item feeds a recipe, investigation or Replicator queue before selling it.
                      • Medications restock medkits: one pile restores 100 charges and takes one slot.
                      • Unlock the Lock actuator board through the Dam "Rosin" Stockpile objective. It opens electric doors on Dam, Dogorsk and Park.
                      • Buy the Keys fuse recipe at Monolith Delta (300 tokens) to roll hangar, attic and crane keys from cassettes.
                      • Rebind the new "reload all magazines" action. It's unbound by default.
